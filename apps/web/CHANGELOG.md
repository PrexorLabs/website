# Changelog

All notable changes to prexorlabs.dev are documented here.
The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Start page on paper: the headline among the lab's own drawings, which drift and give way to the mouse, a fan of
  project cards with the newest release in the middle and its screenshots in turn, and the list of projects.
- Light and dark theme, following the system until a choice in the header; the choice is kept in the browser only.
- The hotbar as main menu with Home, Projects, Discord and GitHub, number keys 1 to 4 as shortcuts.
- Imprint and privacy page, and a 404 page in both languages.
- Fraunces and Instrument Sans, cut to the characters and the one cut the site uses (148 KB to 43 KB).

### Removed

- The inventory start page, the ServerListPlus, lab and donate pages, the lab feed with its Worker, database and
  Discord integration, and the copy of the Prexor design tokens.
